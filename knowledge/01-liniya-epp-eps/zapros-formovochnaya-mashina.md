## Текст запроса (EN — отправлять всем)

**Subject:** RFQ — EPP shape moulding machine, mould size approx. 1400 x 1000 mm

Dear Sir or Madam,

KARKAS PROM (Ukraine) is planning a new EPP moulding line in Western Ukraine and is requesting a commercial offer for a shape moulding machine comparable to Teubert Unimat 140/100.

**Required specification**
- Material: EPP (also EPS as an option), densities approx. 20–80 g/l
- Mould clamping area: approx. 1400 x 1000 mm (please state exact mould size, daylight and stroke)
- Steam pressure suitable for EPP (please state operating range), pressure filling / crack filling
- Vacuum system, mould cooling, quick mould change
- PLC with process data recording and remote monitoring (Ethernet / OPC UA)
- CE conformity

**Please quote separately (if available)**
1. Machine price, EXW and DAP Ukraine (Lviv region)
2. Pressure tank / bead storage for EPP (pressure treatment)
3. Steam boiler / steam accumulator, compressor, cooling water and vacuum units
4. Installation, commissioning and operator training on site
5. Recommended spare parts package for 2 years
6. Refurbished / used machines of this class, if available

**Please also state**
- Lead time and payment terms
- Installed power (kW), steam, air and water consumption per cycle; typical cycle time for EPP
- Behaviour on power loss: can the machine run from a diesel generator; UPS for PLC
- Warranty, remote service, references in Eastern Europe
- Leasing / financing options

The offer is needed for a state grant application, therefore please send it as a PDF on your letterhead, in EUR, valid for at least 90 days, with technical specification.

Thank you in advance.
